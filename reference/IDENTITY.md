# Identity

## Stable identity statement

GAIA is a local-first Personal AI Operating System: a personal ecosystem of specialised digital collaborators that helps its owner reduce cognitive load, coordinate context, and act through explicit capabilities while keeping important decisions under human control.

## What GAIA is

- personal and single-owner first;
- collaborator-based, with bounded responsibilities;
- local-first in ownership, control, and operation where practical;
- human-controlled for sensitive or irreversible outcomes;
- evolvable across models, frameworks, hardware, interfaces, and domains;
- broader than a chat interface.

## What GAIA is not

GAIA is not primarily a chatbot, generic agent framework, workflow product, home automation dashboard, model-provider wrapper, cloud assistant, plugin collection, or replacement for human judgement.

## Identity tests

1. **Chat replacement:** GAIA still makes sense without chat.
2. **Framework replacement:** replacing the framework does not replace GAIA.
3. **Model replacement:** changing provider preserves identity.
4. **Channel replacement:** replacing Telegram preserves behaviour and policy.
5. **Local-first:** meaningful value remains when external services are unavailable.
6. **Human control:** important behaviour can be understood, approved, corrected, or stopped.
7. **Memory intent:** retained information has reason, provenance, correction, and forgetting paths.
8. **Complexity:** one human maintainer with AI collaborators can understand and operate it.
9. **Boundary:** responsibility ownership is explicit.
10. **Identity over implementation:** durable intent wins over tool convenience.

## Review guidance

Change this document rarely. If only implementation knowledge changes, identity probably should not.
